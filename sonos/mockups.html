S-1 through S-5 above. S-5 (split) pairs well with the common "Title - Remastered" or "Title (Live)" pattern — the main title stays large and legible while the qualifier shrinks and dims. S-3 (scroll) feels most "alive" as a screen. S-4 (scale) is the most mechanical but always fits.

02  —  Playback Controls — Dynamic Visibility

hover to reveal
C–2 Auto-hide, tap to show

C–3 Progress-only, expand
tap bar to expand
C–4 Dots bloom to buttons
tap to expand
C–5 Proximity reveal
hover near to reveal

C-1 (ghost) and C-5 (proximity) are natural for mouse use; C-2 (auto-hide) and C-3 (progress-only) feel most at home on iPad where touch is the primary gesture. C-4 (dots bloom) is the most minimalist at rest and most satisfying as an interaction — three dots become three buttons.
