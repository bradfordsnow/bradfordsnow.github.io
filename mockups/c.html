About

Bradford Snow is a Los Angeles-based composer, producer, and multi-instrumentalist whose work spans artist recordings, film and television scoring, and commercial sync.

Scoring, Sound & Picture Film, television, and podcast credits include Tuca & Bertie (Netflix), The Bakken (Amazon Prime), The Flannoisseur, The Deep Vessel, and chart-topping Stassi. He has scored hundreds of TV ads for major global brands (Nike, Google, NBA, etc.), airing across ABC, CBS, NBC, FOX, CW, and Amazon.

Artist Recording & Production His production credits span pop and independent music, including Fox's X Factor (L.A. Reid, Britney Spears, Demi Lovato, John Shanks, The Underdogs, Toby Gad, Fifth Harmony, Camila Cabello), Jon Walker (Panic! at the Disco), Le Swish (The Hush Sound), and Los Angeles Power Disco (Cobra Man).

Based at his Pasadena studio, he works with artists, agencies, and directors from early concept to final master.
