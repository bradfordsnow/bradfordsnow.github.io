About

Bradford Snow is a Los Angeles-based composer, producer, and multi-instrumentalist whose work spans artist recordings, film and television scoring, and commercial sync.

Scoring, Sound & Picture Film, television, video games, and podcast credits include Tuca & Bertie (Netflix), The Bakken (Amazon), The Flannoisseur, The Deep Vessel, and chart-topping Stassi. He has scored hundreds of TV ads for major global brands across all major networks (selected credits below).

Artist Recording & Production His production credits span pop and independent music, including Fox's X Factor (L.A. Reid, Britney Spears, Demi Lovato, John Shanks, The Underdogs, Toby Gad, Fifth Harmony, Camila Cabello), Jon Walker (Panic! at the Disco), Le Swish (The Hush Sound), and Los Angeles Power Disco (Cobra Man). He has also toured as a multi-instrumentalist band member with Juliette Lewis, The Hush Sound, and Company of Thieves.

Based at his Pasadena studio, he works with artists, agencies, and directors from early concept to final master.
