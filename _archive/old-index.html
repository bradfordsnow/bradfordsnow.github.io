Bradford Snow

Bradford Snow is a Los Angeles based music producer, composer, multi-instrumentalist, performer, engineer and musical director. He produces artists and compositions for TV and film at his studio in Pasadena, California.

Bradford's recorded works appear on ABC, CBS, NBC, FOX, CW, Netflix, Amazon, cable networks and internationally. He occasionally tours as a musician with artists such as Juliette Lewis, Company of Thieves, and The Hush Sound; and has worked as a musician and arranger on Fox's X Factor alongside L.A. Reid, John Shanks, The Underdogs, Toby Gad, and other Grammy-winning artists.
